Scope and contact

This policy covers the Memory Music iPhone, iPad and Mac apps and this privacy and support website. Memory Music focuses on organizing and playing local music. Contact the developer about privacy or support at kenapple9527@gmail.com. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

02

Music and local information

You keep control of the music files you select. The app stores song information, source paths, tags, smart collections, playlists, memory notes, listening history, artwork and lyrics locally for organization, playback and on-device suggestions. It does not supply music downloads or upload original audio files to a developer server or CloudKit. Removing a library record and deleting a local file are separate actions; deleting local music requires your explicit selection and confirmation.

03

iCloud synchronization

In versions that support synchronization, the app uses a private Apple CloudKit database to automatically synchronize song identification information (including content hashes, available audio fingerprints and necessary song metadata), tags, notes, playlists, collection rules, listening history, associated artwork and text lyrics across devices using the same iCloud account. Synchronization also uses an app-generated device identifier and version and operation information. Audio files, complete SQLite databases, directory-access bookmarks and absolute local paths are excluded from synchronization payloads. Imported images retain their original file contents and may contain embedded metadata.

04

Apple account and permissions

Your CloudKit information is stored in your private iCloud database and may count toward your iCloud storage. Apple operates the account and service infrastructure. The app does not ask you to give the developer your Apple account password. System pickers grant access to directories and images you select; saved directory permissions allow later access. Background audio and notification capabilities support playback and synchronization. You can manage permissions and iCloud use in system settings.

05

In-app purchases and access

In versions offering Pro, Apple App Store and StoreKit handle subscriptions and the lifetime purchase. The app reads and verifies Apple-signed transactions, product identifiers, expiry and revocation or refund status on the device to unlock features and restore purchases. Apple processes payments and related account information; the developer does not receive card numbers or your Apple account password. The app does not upload receipts, transaction proof or purchase-account information to a developer server or CloudKit, and does not treat an iCloud account as proof of payment. Existing local information is retained after subscription expiry. Restore Purchases uses the system Media & Purchases account. Purchasing is still being prepared and tested for release; availability and actual prices are determined by the Apple store information shown in the app.

06

Online lookups you initiate

When you request metadata or artwork, search information such as song title, artist and album may be sent to MusicBrainz. Artwork is retrieved through Cover Art Archive and its asset-hosting services. Audio identification sends a locally generated fingerprint, duration and your AcoustID Client Key to AcoustID, not the audio file. These services also receive IP addresses and technical request information needed for the connection. Their own policies govern retention and use. You can avoid these requests by not using the corresponding lookup features.

07

Advertising, tracking and support email

The current app does not integrate advertising, cross-app advertising tracking or a developer-operated behavioral analytics service. Suggestions use your information and listening history on the device, not an advertising profile. If you email support, your address, message and attachments reach the developer through email services and are used to handle your request and necessary follow-up. Please do not send passwords, complete private libraries or unnecessary sensitive information.

08

Retention, export and deletion

The app provides backup export and controls for managing or deleting supported records. Deletions require synchronization opportunities to reach other devices. Synchronization history, deletion markers and backups may retain information needed for consistency or recovery; not every copy is guaranteed to disappear immediately. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete iCloud data. You can manage cloud storage using Apple-provided iCloud controls; keep any backups you need before removal. Contact the support email to request access, correction or deletion of information you previously sent by email.

09

This website and third parties

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure may process IP addresses and access logs for service delivery, security and operations. These pages add no advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or registration forms. Support links open your own email app and do not submit messages on this website. Third-party links have their own policies.

We will update this page and its date when features, services or data handling change.
