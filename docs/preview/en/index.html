A FEW MORE THINGS

Good to know.

Does it include music to stream?+

Memory Music plays and organizes local music files you bring. It does not supply a streaming catalog, music downloads or a music subscription.

Does iCloud transfer my audio files?+

Pro syncs organization information such as tags, playlists and music memories, not original audio. Each device needs its own music files. Sync is not a replacement for audio backups.

What happens when a subscription ends?+

Your existing tags, smart collections, memories and other information remain available to use, edit and export. Creating more tags or smart collections above the free limits, Pro sync and CarPlay require active Pro access.

How do I restore Pro on another device?+

Use the Apple Account you purchased with, then choose Restore Purchases in the app. This restores valid Pro access; music files and library information are supplied, synced or restored separately.

Are artwork and fingerprint tools paid?+

Both are free features. Online lookups require a network connection and are subject to third-party availability and terms. Fingerprint lookups may require your own AcoustID Client Key.
