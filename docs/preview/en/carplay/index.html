GOOD TO KNOW

About CarPlay.

Is CarPlay a separate purchase?+

CarPlay is included with Memory Music Pro. Monthly, yearly and lifetime options unlock the same Pro features; no separate CarPlay purchase is needed.

Does music on my Mac automatically appear in my car?+

No. CarPlay uses local audio accessible on the connected iPhone. iCloud syncs organization information, not original audio. Prepare the music on your iPhone and check folder access first.

Does playback always need an internet connection?+

Local music already downloaded to your iPhone and accessible to the app does not depend on a streaming service. Cloud-only, undownloaded or inaccessible files need attention on your iPhone first.

Which vehicles are compatible?+

You need an iPhone and vehicle or head unit compatible with Apple CarPlay. Connection methods and layouts depend on your devices, software and vehicle. See Apple’s setup guide and your vehicle manual.
