01

Bring your own music

In Settings, open Music Sources and choose a local directory with the system picker. Music must be available separately on each device; synchronizing organization data does not transfer audio.

02

Add song information, artwork and lyrics

Song information, artwork and lyrics come from your existing local files or are added by you. The version being prepared for release does not offer online song-information lookup, online artwork lookup or audio fingerprint identification, and sends no queries to those services. Edit song details, choose a local image, or import a local lyrics file on the Lyrics page.

03

A directory or song is unavailable

Check that external storage is connected, files are downloaded and the app still has access. If needed, reselect the same directory in Music Sources. Keep original files and backups; do not begin troubleshooting by deleting the library.

04

Organization data has not synchronized

Check that devices use the same iCloud account, have connectivity and available iCloud storage. Review the organization synchronization status in Settings. Background opportunities can be delayed; open the app and check again. A device without the audio files will not receive them automatically.

05

Backup and restore

Export information using Data Backup in Settings. Review the preview and checks before restoring and confirm the target. Backups contain organization data and supported user resources. Synchronization is not a backup of original audio files.

06

Report an issue

Email the app version, device and OS version, reproduction steps, and expected versus actual results. Remove private information from screenshots. Do not send Apple account passwords, a complete database or your music directory.

07

Availability and pricing

The app is being prepared for release. This website does not sell licenses or take payments and has no live App Store download link. Availability and pricing will be shown on the App Store when the app is released.
